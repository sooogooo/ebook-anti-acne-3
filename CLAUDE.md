## Project Overview

This repository contains a comprehensive Chinese ebook about acne treatment written from a dermatologist's perspective. The main file is `anti-acne.md` (2897 lines, 330KB), which serves as a complete guide to understanding, treating, and managing acne.

## Content Structure

The ebook is organized into three main parts:

**Part 1: Understanding the Problem**
- Chapter 1: Professional diagnosis of different acne types
- Chapter 2: Psychological aspects of acne treatment

**Part 2: Medical Treatment Approaches**
- Chapter 3: Oral and topical medications
- Chapter 4: Advanced cosmetic treatments (light therapy, microneedling)
- Chapter 5: Author's proprietary methods - "Sandwich Therapy" and "Combination Therapy"

**Part 3: Holistic Self-Care**
- Chapter 6: Scientific skincare routines
- Chapter 7: Anti-inflammatory diet guidance
- Chapter 8: Mental wellness approaches ("Three Positives" method)
- Chapter 9-12: FAQ, case studies, prevention strategies, and encouragement

**Appendices and Supplements:**
- Quick reference guides for medications and ingredients
- Hospital and resource recommendations
- Glossary of terms
- Self-tracking templates
- AI and future treatment perspectives
